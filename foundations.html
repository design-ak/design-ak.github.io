Foundations

The tokens every screen and component is built from. Names match Figma one to one, so text/secondary in Figma is --text-secondary on the web and context.colors.textSecondary in Flutter.

Colour tokens

Use these in designs and code. Each swatch shows Light on the left and Dark on the right; tap one to copy its CSS variable. In Flutter read them as context.colors.<name>.

Typography

Roboto for English, Noto Sans Gujarati and Noto Sans Devanagari as fallbacks, in 24 styles.

Languages

One font stack covers all three scripts. Mixed text such as “Satsang — સત્સંગ” needs no per-word font change; each character picks the right font automatically.

English — Roboto
Satsang

Join the Sunday satsang at 7:00 PM. Mark your attendance at the door.

ગુજરાતી — Noto Sans Gujarati
સત્સંગ

રવિવારે સાંજે 7:00 વાગ્યે સત્સંગમાં જોડાઓ. દરવાજે તમારી હાજરી નોંધાવો.
